# Azrak the Wall-Eater

Azrak does not eat food.

Azrak eats **structures**.

Concrete.  
Drywall.  
The concept of shelter.

## Lore

When humans invented houses, Azrak woke up.

Every abandoned building is believed to be a corpse.
